# Weekly planner

Choose **Week planner** in the web work-order screen to see a Monday-to-Sunday schedule. Each day's count includes the saved orders that match your search and status filter. Managers see workspace records; workers see their own assignments. Sync first when you need recent server changes.

Choose a day to narrow the work-order list, or choose **Whole week** to restore all seven days. **Previous week** and **Next week** move by seven calendar days. **This week** returns to the current local week and clears the selected day. Today is labeled separately from the selected day.

Planner lists are ordered by due date, then work-order reference. The ordinary List keeps its existing order.

Managers can select a day, then choose **Assign for selected day**. The existing assignment form opens with that due date filled in; it can still be changed before submitting. Without a selected day, new assignments default to the device's local date. Creation requires a connection and available sites, checklists, and workers; a failed submission leaves the form open for correction or retry.

The planner works offline with saved records. Browsing does not change reports, drafts, or the outbox. Dates remain calendar dates across time zones and daylight-saving changes. The displayed week stays where you left it across midnight; the Today marker updates, and This week returns to the new current week.

Search and status apply to both List and Week planner. The list's custom date range is preserved while the planner uses its own week/day window. Switching back restores the custom range. Opening an order and returning keeps the planner selection; reloading or signing out resets view/filter selections.

Manager CSV exports follow the current week or selected day, plus search and status. The footer reports the filtered order count; In the field and Ready for review remain workspace summaries. Empty-day and empty-week messages suggest changing the date or filters.

The planner schedules new assignments. Managers can also open unfinished orders and [change their due dates](rescheduling.md); returning to the planner preserves its selected day and reflects the new date. Drag-and-drop, recurring work, and native calendar controls remain roadmap work.
